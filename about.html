About FrostLink

A more connected way to source frozen food

FrostLink exists to make one part of the frozen food supply chain simpler: connecting the people who make frozen products with the businesses that need to buy them in quantity.

Frozen food procurement for hotels, restaurants, food-service companies and distributors typically runs through scattered contacts, phone calls and one-off introductions. FrostLink is built as a B2B network to make that process more direct — a place where manufacturers can present their products and capabilities, and buyers can find and enquire about what they need.

FrostLink's focus is frozen fruits, frozen vegetables and related frozen food products. The platform is designed around how commercial frozen food sourcing actually works: bulk quantities, cold-chain logistics, and direct business-to-business communication — not a retail shopping experience.

Every enquiry submitted through FrostLink — whether a product quote request, a manufacturer registration, or a commercial sourcing enquiry — is reviewed and submitted by the person filling it in, and routed to the relevant business party for direct follow-up.

Manufacturer-first listings

Products are always shown with their manufacturer, so buyers know who they'd be sourcing from.

Built for bulk

Every form on FrostLink is designed around commercial quantities, delivery locations and logistics needs.

Direct communication

Enquiries are prepared clearly and routed straight to the relevant manufacturer or buyer contact.

What FrostLink Is Not

Not a retail store. Not a marketplace checkout.

There's no shopping cart on FrostLink, and no online payment. Every product listing leads to a quote request, and every enquiry is completed and submitted by the visitor themselves through Google Forms — FrostLink prepares the request, the person submits it. This keeps the process transparent and puts the buyer or manufacturer in control of what's shared.
